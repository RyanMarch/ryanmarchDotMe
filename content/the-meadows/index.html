The Meadows

Website, Branding & Print Relaunch

Web Designer & Marketing Lead • 2014

About The Meadows

The Meadows Banquet Hall is an established event and catering venue in Cincinnati, Ohio featuring banquet buffet facilities and full-service dining. I worked closely with the business owner on a complete brand relaunch including web design, print collateral, and business booking operations.

The entrance sign at The Meadows Banquet Hall.

When I began the project, the venue’s existing web presence was limited and outdated. The websit was difficult to navigate on mobile devices. The venue also lacked a unified brand identity across its print marketing and booking documents.

The goal was to build a cohesive digital and print ecosystem to showcase the venue's elegant spaces and drive community bookings.

Before: The original desktop layout, which lacked mobile optimization.

Website Redesign & Development

I redesigned and developed a modern, mobile-friendly WordPress site at themeadowsbanquet.com. The new site prioritized high-resolution photography, event listings, and an interactive menu builder to help prospective clients plan their events.

After: The fully responsive, modern WordPress website homepage.
The redesigned website in action on a laptop, optimized for modern displays.

Print Design & Direct Mail

To drive physical foot traffic to the venue's public Monday Night Buffets and seasonal events (such as the Western Hills Bridal Fair), I designed cohesive print materials and direct-mail assets:

  • Direct Mail Campaigns: Designed and formatted large-format direct mail postcards compliant with USPS Every Door Direct Mail (EDDM) specifications to target local postal routes.
  • Promotional Flyers: Created print-ready layouts for seasonal celebrations, local music showcases, and weekly buffet promotions.
  • Logo Optimization: Cleaned up and vectorized their brand logo for use across print, direct mailers, and web icons.
Direct Mailer: The custom print mailer designed for local neighborhood outreach campaigns.
The Meadows direct mail postcard back design.

Business Operations & Photography

To support the digital launch, I overhauled the venue’s client booking pipeline and captured updated photography. Additional efforts included:

  • Standardized Contracts: Created a unified, professional event contract template to standardize client bookings, deposits, and billing rules.
  • Catering Menu Architecture: Structured and cataloged their wide range of menus (from specialty buffets to formal sit-down dinners) to make options clear for prospective customers.
  • Venue & Detail Photography: Shot high-resolution interior and tabletop photography to share the look and feel of each unique space and dining experience.
