This mashup brings Iyaz and Hoobastank together...against better judgement.

The Project

These two songs have the same bpm and are the relative major/minor pair. Surely that's not all that's needed for a good mashup?

Download MP3

Mixing & Production

I tracked down an instrumental for Hoobastank's "Crawling in the Dark" and the acapella for Iyaz's "Replay". Musically, they line up better than they should. "Replay" is in A major and "Crawling in the Dark" is in F♯ minor. Because F♯ minor is the relative minor of A major, both songs share the exact same scale and notes, so no pitch shifting was needed.

"Replay" sits right at 92 BPM locked to a grid, while "Crawling in the Dark" has a range of 92–94 BPM and syncopated drumming. Dropping the vocal straight in, again, worked better than it should have. But it wasn't a perfect fit. So, I made heavy use of Logic Pro's Flex Time to warp Iyaz's phrasing, sometimes down to a word-by-word level. The focus was making the vocal feel and sound natural rather than forcing it onto an exact grid.

I added some delay, reverb, and a few transition effects to bridge sections together and help the clean vocal sit naturally against the heavy guitars. The whole thing was put together quickly since it's fundamentally a joke, but dialing in the vocal timing makes it work.
