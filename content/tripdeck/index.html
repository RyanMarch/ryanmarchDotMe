The Product

TripDeck is a beautiful, easy-to-use, collaborative travel itinerary dashboard designed to make planning trips fast, flexible, and stress-free. It turns flight confirmations, train bookings, hotel reservations, and loose travel ideas into an organized, interactive timeline. As plans take shape, TripDeck automatically pulls in flight routes, train stations, venue addresses, and even hourly weather forecasts - keeping everything you need in one place, even when adventuring completely off the grid.

Most travel apps force an awkward compromise: either rigid, form-heavy tools that stop making sense for informal plans, or open-ended note apps that lack real-time utility. TripDeck delivers the best of both worlds. Enjoy the freedom of a scratchpad with the intelligence and clarity of a live travel dashboard.

TripDeck is built around ensuring privacy: itineraries are encrypted directly on device so no one else can read them. Build entire trips without even creating an account. Work seamlessly online and offline, and share clean itineraries with other travel companions.

Key Features

  • Dual-Pane Workspace: Edit itineraries using a visual drag-and-drop outline or a clean markdown editor. Changes in either pane update the other instantly.
  • Multi-Modal Transit & Flights: Type a flight number or search train and transit lines to automatically fetch routes, departure coordinates, and scheduled times.
  • Find & Add Places: Search for hotels, restaurants, and sights to instantly drop in addresses, map pins, and local details.
  • Scheduled Events & Flexible Ideas: Organize time-bound events alongside unscheduled activities and a dedicated ideas scratchpad for things you might want to do later.
  • Interactive Route Map: Automatically plots your full journey including air routes, transit connections, accommodations, and activities.

Zero-Knowledge Sharing & Security: Share travel plans without sacrificing personal privacy or exposing sensitive confirmation codes:

  • Zero-Friction Passkey Sign-in: Sign in with Face ID, Touch ID, or Windows Hello across your devices without passwords, PINs, or forgettable credentials.
  • View-Only Sharing: Generates secure links that automatically redact sensitive confirmation numbers, gate codes, and private notes.

  • Instant Revocation: Rotating or generating a new share link revokes previous links immediately.

Product Strategy & Leadership

As the product manager and creator of TripDeck, I led the product from initial discovery through user experience design, system architecture, and launch.

  • User Problem Framing: Analyzed why travelers still rely heavily on spreadsheets and unstructured documents despite dozens of travel apps. Identified the core unmet need: people want structured travel plans that work them both before and during their trip.
  • Frictionless Onboarding Strategy: Designed an instantly-accessible "Guest Mode" where users can immediately start typing or pasting plans without hitting a registration wall. Trip data saves directly to the browser, proving value before ever asking for user commitment.
  • Multi-Modal Transit Expansion: Extended itinerary intelligence beyond basic restaurants and activities to include flights, trains, subways, and public transit systems, creating a unified door-to-door journey planner.
  • Designing for the Travel Mindset: Structured the UI around high-stress travel moments: a Next Milestone countdown ticker, automatic timezone conversions, and offline accessibility for spotty connectivity in transit.
  • Privacy by Design as a Value Proposition: Positioned user privacy as a central product pillar. By keeping user data encrypted on the client and supporting effortless passkeys, TripDeck delivers peace of mind while eliminating backend data liability.

Technical Architecture

TripDeck is built as a modern web application designed for speed, portability, and zero-maintenance reliability:

  • Vanilla Web Stack: Built with native ES6 JavaScript modules, modern CSS custom properties, and semantic HTML operating without heavy frontend frameworks or compilation build steps.
  • Serverless Cloudflare Backend: Deployed on Cloudflare with lightweight functions that securely proxy real-time flight data, Transitland rail systems, Google Places, Mapbox GL maps, and Open-Meteo weather APIs.
  • Client-Side End-to-End Encryption: Itinerary data is encrypted on the client device before syncing to cloud storage (Cloudflare D1), deriving master keys directly from WebAuthn passkeys so that server infrastructure never sees raw travel details.
  • Offline Caching & Conflict-Safe Sync: A Service Worker pre-caches application assets for offline travel, while a conflict-safe concurrency queue prevents data loss when making edits across multiple browser tabs or spotty connections.

Learnings & Takeaways

Building TripDeck reinforced several key product principles: reducing friction upfront drives engagement, thoughtful defaults outperform complex settings menus, and privacy does not have to come at the expense of a collaborative user experience.

By treating travel planning as an iterative, fluid process supporting everything from flight confirmations to half-formed itinerary ideas, TripDeck provides travelers with clarity when planning at home and confidence while on the move.
