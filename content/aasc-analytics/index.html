The Project

The American Association of Service Coordinators (AASC) is a national professional membership association representing over 4,000 service coordinators in affordable housing communities. Their web portal is the central hub for member resources, education, and advocacy.

After launching a new site, we overhauled their web analytics and search visibility, giving their marketing team accurate data on how members interact with the site.

Home page of the AASC site.

The Challenge

AASC's digital ecosystem had a few tracking gaps and search indexing challenges. Because their team is fully remote, standard IP address filtering could not exclude internal testing traffic, necessitating a URL visit-based tracking strategy.

Additionally, their CMS returned a 200 OK server status for broken links instead of a true 404 Not Found response. This "Soft 404" anomaly caused search engines to spend crawl budget indexing thousands of pages at the expense of high-value member resources.

Reviewing page performance in Google Search Console.

Key Takeaways

  • Analytics Configuration: Integrated Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4, along with filtering out remote staff traffic.
  • Custom Event Tracking: Built triggers for header dropdown navigation clicks to reveal popular menu items, and configured scroll depth tracking to measure reading engagement on long-form content.
  • Search Intent: Mapped the site search into GA4 so the team can see exactly what members are searching for.
  • SEO Audit & Cleanup: Identified over 11,000 pages in Google Search index,mapping redirects and drafting robots.txt rules to block legacy directories and URLs.

My Role & Impact

As the Technical Consultant, I owned the analytics strategy, implementation, and technical SEO audit. I worked closely with AASC's marketing leadership to deliver:

  • Search Authority Fix: Cleaned up the Soft 404 issues that were wasting Google's crawl budget on 11,000+ broken pages, helping their actual content get indexed properly.
  • Clean Member Journey Data: Revealing how members used and moved through the site and its resources.
  • Content Engagement Funnels: Provided the team with scroll and search metrics, allowing them to optimize their long-form resources and spot content gaps.
  • Handover & Training: Built a custom GA4 exploration dashboard, shared clean templates for future projects, and trained the team to manage everything internally.
