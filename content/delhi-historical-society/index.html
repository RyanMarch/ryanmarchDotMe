Delhi Historical Society

Website Audit, Analytics, Google Workspace

Consultant • 2025–2026

The Project

The Delhi Historical Society is a local nonprofit preserving the history of Delhi, Ohio. I was brought in to audit their website, establish web analytics, migrate the organization to Google Workspace for Nonprofits, and help launch an interactive community program.

The Work

  • Website Audit: Identified broken links, mobile usability issues, image performance needs, and gaps in page metadata.
  • Google Analytics 4: Configured GA4 and Google Tag Manager, including event tracking.
  • Google Search Console: Verified the domain, submitted a sitemap, and reviewed index coverage to identify orphaned and duplicate pages.
  • Google Workspace Migration: Secured nonprofit eligibility, configured DNS and email authentication, provisioned accounts, and migrated mailboxes.
  • Find the Founders: Helped launch an interactive real-world scavenger hunt activity.
The Delhi Historical Society website.

My Role & Impact

  • Delivered analytics and search visibility for the organization.
  • Provided zero-downtime migration to Google Workspace.
  • Assisted in the launch of Find the Founders, a community scavenger hunt connecting residents with local history.
