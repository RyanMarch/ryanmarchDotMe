CLEAR Tech

Business Plan & Branding

Creative Technologist & Planner • March 2015

The Venture

CLEAR Tech was conceived as a comprehensive business plan and branding suite for a mobile audio recording and media production service based in Columbus, Ohio.

The core service focused on providing professional, high-fidelity live recording services to student performing ensembles (such as high school concert bands, choirs, and orchestras) with zero financial drain on school resources. By offering live recording and event production to the schools for free, the business model relied on selling archival-quality audio CDs directly to parents, students, and educators immediately following the performance.

The flat, minimalist CLEAR Tech brand logo.

The Marketing & Sales Model

To cut costs and minimize turnaround time, CLEAR Tech bypassed traditional fundraising channels. Traditional mobile recording companies relied on selling directly to booster groups, shifting the logistical burden of marketing, sales, and order fulfillment onto volunteers.

CLEAR Tech’s strategy simplified this process: with school approval, the team handled all recording, manufacturing, on-site sales, and distribution. Booster groups assumed no financial risk, while receiving a free archival-quality copy for school records.

Tri-fold marketing brochure showcasing the brand design and service offerings.

Operations & Setup Costs

The business plan detailed the operational workflow, including projecting financial tables, marketing research, and equipment logistics:

  • Field Operations & Sales: Travel to school auditoriums, set up stereo microphone arrays, manage recording sessions, and handle CD pre-orders and sales directly in the lobby post-performance.
  • Post-Production & Fulfillment: Mix and master the multi-channel recordings, burn/duplicate media, print custom labels, and coordinate delivery back to the client schools.
  • Equipment Setup: The plan proposed a lean hardware configuration consisting of high-end condenser microphones, multi-channel interfaces, digital audio workstation (DAW) software, and a custom high-capacity CD duplicator and label printer.
