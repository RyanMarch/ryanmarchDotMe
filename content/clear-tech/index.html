CLEAR Tech

Business Plan & Branding

Creative Technologist & Planner • March 2015

Overview

CLEAR Tech was conceived as a comprehensive business plan for a mobile audio recording and media production service based in Columbus, Ohio.

The core service focused on providing professional, high-fidelity live recording services to student performing ensembles such as high school concert bands, choirs, and orchestras with no financial cost on school resources. By offering live recording and event production to the schools for free, the business model relied on selling archival-quality audio CDs directly to parents, students, and educators immediately following the performance.

The Product

To cut costs and minimize turnaround time, CLEAR Tech bypassed traditional fundraising channels. Other mobile recording companies relied on selling directly to booster groups, shifting the burden of marketing, sales, and order fulfillment onto volunteers.

CLEAR Tech’s strategy simplified this process: with school approval, the team handled all recording, manufacturing, on-site sales, and distribution. Booster groups assumed no financial risk and received a free archival-quality copy for school records.

Operations

The business plan detailed the operational workflow, equipment configurations, and delivery logistics:

  • Field Operations & Sales: Travel to venues, set up microphone arrays, manage recording sessions, and handle CD sales directly in the lobby.
  • Equipment Setup: Deploy a lean hardware configuration consisting of high-end condenser microphones, multi-channel interfaces, and DAW software.
  • Post-Production & Fulfillment: Mix and master the multi-channel recordings, duplicate media, print custom labels, and coordinate delivery.
  • Business Administration: An Operations Manager oversaw school outreach, client relations, scheduling, marketing campaigns, and bookkeeping from a dedicated Columbus workspace.

Projections & Growth

The financial forecasting and marketing strategy targeted a sustainable launch model and future capabilities:

  • Market Analysis: Target regional Columbus school music programs, establishing a zero-risk direct-to-consumer model that bypasses traditional booster-managed pre-orders in favor of lobby sales.
  • Financial Projections: Secure a $75,000 initial startup investment, with detailed projections showing profitability in about 14 months and full payoff in 36 months.
  • Future Growth: Expand service offerings over time to encompass online digital distribution, high-definition concert videography, and concurrent field recording crews.
